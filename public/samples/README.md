# Offline demo samples

The grape leaf-blight and tomato yellow-leaf-curl sample photographs are copied from the PlantVillage full multi-crop re-release (`fatemehENG/plantvillage-full`), whose dataset card states CC0 1.0 inherited from the upstream PlantVillage release. They are demonstration examples only, photographed against controlled backgrounds; they are not Syrian field photographs. Cite Mohanty, Hughes & Salathé (2016), DOI 10.3389/fpls.2016.01419.
